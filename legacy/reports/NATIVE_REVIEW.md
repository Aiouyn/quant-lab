## Participant authority

- The scientist receives verified native facts and may commit a `MEASURED_VENUE` stop using exact supplied replay IDs. It may instead report that the evidence is insufficient; such prose does not fabricate a completed decision.
- The falsifier independently assesses the stop and must return a structured support/disagree/insufficient verdict with observations citing exact supplied replay IDs, counterarguments, limitations and a next experiment. The runtime rejects an empty or incorrectly sourced critique. Completion does not imply agreement or simulator certification.
- The historian receives the critique from the completed durable falsifier task, rather than a caller's replacement narrative. It records the scoped lesson through the existing immutable service. Disagreement and full trusted grounding remain in the stored lesson.

`quantdesk/mission_review.py` constructs context from verified local reports and their four bound journals. It checks immutable candidate identity, registered measured evidence, the current campaign/cycle and source scope before a provider reservation. Caller-supplied numerical summaries are replaced by local facts. Repeated versions of an economic configuration cannot count as separate review observations. The ordinary scientist and engineer retain their broader investigation/build tools and numerical contracts.

The profile narrows advertised tools and the native-stop schema. Runtime checks also enforce its exact IDs; schema advertisement alone is not enforcement. Review task replay/decision scopes are frozen across checkpoint reuse. Completed decisions and lessons recover without another summarization call. No private reasoning is persisted, and MiMo thinking remains enabled in requests.

Ordinary company native-stop cycles automatically use the focused falsifier/historian profile. An explicitly scoped existing-result scientist task uses:
