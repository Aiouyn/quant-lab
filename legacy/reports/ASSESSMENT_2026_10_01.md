## Proposed evidence required before a long autonomous allocation

These are acceptance requirements, not achieved results or promises of alpha:

1. Run a small predeclared real cohort batch under a frozen total allowance. Each mission must yield either a reproducible candidate with complete development replay and matched comparisons, or an independently reviewed evidence-based stop. Record operational failures separately. Require consecutive completed missions and a stop/restart recovery without repeated completed work before allocating many hours.
2. Measure charged tokens, elapsed time, manual interventions, repeated deterministic errors and valid economic comparisons per completed mission. Correct handoff/engineering failures before scaling inference spend. Do not replenish the existing campaign silently.
3. Independently reconstruct selected numerical, trade and accounting results. Align historical and target-venue fill assumptions, measure timing/depth sensitivity and document remaining data limitations. A public-book diagnostic cannot calibrate own execution.
4. Freeze surviving code, model, costs and confirmation policy before validation/holdout. Preserve global exposure and trial accounting. Report negative and zero-trade results, with scope, instead of optimizing the visible confirmation set repeatedly.
5. Compare memory-enabled research against a budget-matched control on the same permitted information, with completion/duplication and independently confirmed outcome measures declared in advance.
6. Obtain each surviving strategy's precommitted sustained Hyperliquid shadow record, then form a constrained portfolio and obtain fresh portfolio observations. Existing default seven-day/50-trade forward gates are allocation heuristics, not institutional significance or live-capital approval.

The present best use of the system is supervised, bounded research and reproducible elimination of unsupported ideas. It has meaningful pieces of the requested architecture, but the empirical standard for an autonomous quant desk has not been met.
