# Real feature research and paper execution — 30 September 2026

The system now has measured evidence for this path: real MiMo-authored feature code, independent full-day fitting/replay, a frozen exploratory Hyperliquid bundle, real public-market inference, a real falsifier critique, and a stored grounded lesson. This does not establish profitable alpha, effective repeated learning, a complete autonomous-company acceptance run or fund performance.

## Implementation improvements

- Engineers retain implementation tools on their final turns. Historians retain lesson tools. A prose summary cannot falsely complete either artifact obligation.
- Public program attempts survive rejected parsing and worker failures. Resumption uses task-scoped evidence and exact drafts instead of adopting unrelated campaign candidates. Opaque MiMo reasoning stays ephemeral.
- Failed and interrupted tasks have process identity and terminal states. Live ownership cannot be overwritten, and dead processes are not displayed as active work.
- Retrieval defaults to focused summaries. Actual worker preflight checks causality, bounds, schema and declared inputs before expensive fitting. Prediction diagnostics distinguish weak forecasts, modeled costs, extra edge margin, warmup and funding gates.
- Fitted development artifacts can be exported for exploratory paper testing. Their validation and holdout summaries remain empty; their development evidence is labeled separately.
- Development coverage is checked against global recorded exposure. An agent's recommendation that previously trained dates are fresh is preserved as authored and accompanied by a measured correction.
- Only lessons with measured grounding count toward the research map. A stored lesson is still not proof that later decisions or performance improve.
- Paper records inference timing and separate feed/strategy/stale-decision errors. New orders use measured signal availability plus modeled routing latency. Slow inference halts and preserves explicitly marked exposure rather than fabricating a close at a stale price. Historical replay still assumes immediate feature availability followed by configured latency; it is not calibrated to the entire observed live timing distribution.

## Economic evidence

The feature program passed full-day prefix checks on 86,400 observations. Independent outer replay used 2025-11-11, 2025-11-15 and 2025-11-20, each with 86,400 observations and strictly earlier model training.

The submitted model and the all-custom-features-removed ablation both made zero trades and earned zero PnL. The matched fixed trading baseline lost approximately 1,501.89 in simulated account currency. With zero trades, the custom-feature net ablation is uninformative. No promotion or holdout exposure occurred.

The submitted `min_edge_bps=10.5` is an extra margin after modeled costs, so its actual gross hurdle was approximately 20.5 bps. The rationale confused those semantics; prompts and schema descriptions now state them explicitly. On the first two outer dates, no predictions exceeded modeled costs alone. On the third, ten did; one exceeded the extra margin, but it was inside the funding exclusion window. Removing safeguards is not a justified conclusion.

The real falsifier and historian completed their tasks and recorded negative evidence and a proposed next experiment. The falsifier's suggested fresh dates included previously trained dates. The exposure checker verified that error, and the correction entered organizational evidence. Narrative claims about permanent non-viability or an entire feature class remain hypotheses, not deterministic results.

## Hyperliquid integration and timing

The first public-market probe completed 120 fresh observations and 90 frozen-model inferences, with zero feed/strategy errors and no trades. Its timing journal measured median inference duration near 688 ms and median source-quote-to-decision delay near 1,357 ms. That exposed optimistic scheduling from the quote timestamp before the decision was available.

After correcting scheduling and stale-decision handling, a second probe completed 90 observations and 60 inferences, with zero feed errors, strategy errors or stale decisions. It also made no trades. Both submitted zero exchange orders. These short probes verify integration and timing instrumentation; they provide no fill calibration, profitability or portfolio evidence.

The current full regression suite passed: **145 tests in 60.60 seconds**. Synthetic adversarial tests cover decision availability, funding-hour boundaries, stale inference and stale worker failures, in addition to the fitting, input, budget, checkpoint and promotion boundaries. These tests do not independently certify the simulator.
