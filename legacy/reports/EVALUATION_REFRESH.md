This is the assessment before subsequent screening/recovery improvements and the forward-before-portfolio gate. Follow-up implementation and the failed real pilot are recorded in `AUTONOMOUS_PILOT.md`; current forward acceptance is documented in `FORWARD_ACCEPTANCE.md`. ## Verdict

The system can produce executable agent-authored research candidates, fit models using earlier observations, and replay them with meaningful causal and economic safeguards. It has not demonstrated dependable unattended discovery, profitable alpha, beneficial repeated organizational learning, calibrated Hyperliquid execution, or a validated portfolio. Several hours of operation can produce research evidence; there is no measured basis for predicting that they will produce a tradable system.

This assessment checks current code, durable registry state, live processes, and real verification receipts. It supersedes the current-state claims in the earlier `CURRENT_EVALUATION.md`; that file remains a historical snapshot. No new provider campaign or trading session was launched for this assessment.

## What several hours of research could realistically accomplish

When an explicit bounded research job is started, the director, scientist and engineer can inspect evidence, select an experiment, write supported numerical code, test it, and commit a candidate. Deterministic services fit/replay it; the falsifier and historian critique and retain results. Checkpoints and budgets constrain the run. Negative economic findings can be useful when they resolve a specific hypothesis.

Completion is not yet reliable enough to promise a useful output rate. The real feature task needed recovery from an unsupported operation, a truncated completion, a provider deadline, and a reservation cap before successful submission. Its campaign used 192,716 charged/reserved tokens for 166,380 known actual tokens, including the subsequent critique and lesson. That is measured prototype effort, not an established efficient research throughput.

The submitted five-feature quadratic-ridge system passed full-day worker checks and independent outer replay on November 11, 15 and 20. Each outer fit used strictly earlier training. It made **zero trades and zero PnL** across all three dates. Its no-custom-feature ablation also made zero trades, so that comparison does not establish feature value. The fixed trading comparator lost approximately 1,501.89 simulated account currency. Beating an actively losing comparator by remaining in cash is not positive alpha.

The forecast diagnostics explain the inactivity: most predictions were below modeled costs, and the additional configured margin raised the gross hurdle to about 20.5 bps. One observation cleared that hurdle but was excluded by the funding guard. The agents initially confused extra margin with total cost. The actual critic also suggested previously trained dates as fresh. A measured correction is retained; a persuasive agent critique cannot substitute for checking its claims.

## Backtests: credible safeguards, incomplete realism

The replay is substantially more defensible than an agent returning a claimed Sharpe ratio. Numerical code, observations, fits, assumptions and trade logs are inspectable. Generated code cannot fit on validation or holdout through the supported fitting service. Promotion is deterministic rather than granted by the critic.

However, the broker fills a taker quantity at the best price plus fixed slippage without walking available depth. It does not establish capacity or partial-fill behavior. Historical replay schedules decisions from the quote timestamp; the corrected forward path includes measured inference availability. Historical and forward computation timing therefore remain mismatched. Funding is excluded with entry guards rather than modeled from settlement evidence. Maker results are explicitly an unverified trade-through proxy.

These are material economic limits. A positive replay would justify further verification, not establish executable profit.

The current 4.5 bps taker fee assumption corresponds to the published base perpetual tier: [Hyperliquid fees](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees). Actual account tiers/discounts need their own evidence. Hourly funding is documented in [Hyperliquid funding](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/funding); avoiding many boundaries is not equivalent to accounting for funding. Target book modes are documented in [WebSocket subscriptions](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/websocket/subscriptions).

The two short real paper probes made zero trades and submitted zero exchange orders. The later probe completed 90 observations and 60 inferences with no recorded feed, strategy or stale-decision errors. This verifies a functioning integration path under those observed conditions. It supplies no fill, profit or portfolio evidence.

## Work that changes readiness

1. **Make development screening precede expensive confirmation.** Precommit a small set of full development dates; verify activity, modeled-cost feasibility and matched controls before using the validation period. Preserve useful negative findings and keep operational failures separate. Passing this screen should authorize confirmation, never promotion.
2. **Complete repeated real unattended cycles.** Measure submitted and independently replayable artifacts, useful falsifications, retries, repeated work, token cost and terminal states. Diagnose the two preparation failures and ensure dashboard-launched jobs import current code; the existing server processes predate recent changes.
3. **Establish organizational learning experimentally.** Compare equivalent budget runs with and without retrieved lessons, using precommitted fresh assessment periods and the same gates. Record decision changes and avoided redundant experiments as well as economic outcomes.
4. **Calibrate and independently reconcile execution.** Replay target-venue observations with measured decision delays, depth-aware fills, account units and capacity limits. Independently calculate accounting, fees and applicable funding. Positive historical results must survive those assumptions.
5. **Enforce the intended promotion sequence.** Full validation → untouched sealed holdout → qualifying sustained Hyperliquid shadow evidence → portfolio construction → separate fresh portfolio forward assessment. Historical PAPER_READY should not automatically satisfy the forward evidence step.

The current foundation is useful and several important components have real integration evidence. The ambitious acceptance criteria are not all met. The broad improvement goal remains open; readiness should advance on measured results rather than hours elapsed, agent activity or candidate count.
